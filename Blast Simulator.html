ZONE CONSEQUENCES FOR HUMAN LIFE
FIREBALL (0-100% VAPORIZATION)

Temperatures reach tens of millions of degrees. Human bodies, buildings, and ground vaporize instantly into superheated plasma.

HEAVY BLAST (20 PSI OVERPRESSURE)

Concrete multi-story buildings collapse. Fatal human lung rupture, internal organ crush, 95%+ lethality rate.

MODERATE BLAST (5 PSI OVERPRESSURE)

Residential wood/brick homes collapse. Shattered glass shards travel at hurricane speeds causing severe lacerations and trauma.

THERMAL FLASH (3RD DEGREE BURNS)

Thermal pulse chars skin layers through nerve tissue. Clothes ignite spontaneously; blinding eye damage to anyone facing ground zero.

HUMANITARIAN & SURVIVAL PROTOCOL
1. THE INITIAL 15 SECONDS

Duck & Cover: Never look at the flash (causes permanent retinal blindness). Immediately drop face down, protect exposed skin, and cover head.

2. SHELTER IN PLACE (24-HOUR RULE)

Go to the center of a dense concrete building or subterranean basement. Fallout radiation drops by 90% after 7 hours and 99% after 48 hours.

3. DECONTAMINATION

Remove outer clothing layers (removes ~90% of radioactive dust). Gently wash skin and hair with clean water without scratching.
